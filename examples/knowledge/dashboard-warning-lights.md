# Dashboard warning lights

Fictional example for testing the document index. It describes an invented truck fleet and is not real vehicle guidance.

The instrument cluster of every truck in the example fleet uses three colors. Red means stop as soon as it is safe. Yellow (amber) means the truck can keep driving, but the fault must be checked soon. Green and blue lights only show that a function is on.

## Red lights

### Brake system warning

A red brake symbol with an exclamation mark means low air pressure in the brake circuits or a fault in the braking system. Stop at the next safe place, set the parking brake and do not drive on. Check the air pressure gauges: if the pressure stays below the marked line with the engine running, report a breakdown to the fleet desk.

### Engine oil pressure

A red oil can means the engine has lost oil pressure. Stop the engine at once to avoid serious damage. Wait a few minutes, check the oil level with the dipstick and top up if it is low. Do not restart the engine if the light comes back; report a breakdown.

### Coolant temperature

A red thermometer means the engine is overheating. Stop safely, let the engine idle for a minute and then switch it off. Never open the coolant cap while the engine is hot.

## Yellow lights

### Engine check light

A yellow engine symbol means the engine control unit has stored a fault, for example in the fuel system, the sensors or the exhaust treatment. The truck can usually be driven to the end of the trip at reduced speed. Avoid heavy loads and steep climbs where possible, note when the light came on and whether the engine feels different, and book a workshop visit within the next two working days. If the light flashes, power is reduced or the engine runs roughly, stop and call the fleet desk.

### Tire pressure warning

A yellow tire symbol means at least one tire has lost pressure. See the tire pressure guide for what to do.

### Exhaust fluid warning

A yellow fluid symbol means the exhaust fluid (DEF) tank is running low or the exhaust treatment has a fault. See the exhaust fluid guide.

## Green and blue lights

Green lights show that the indicators, the cruise control or the retarder are on. The blue light shows the main beam. These lights need no action.

## When in doubt

If a light is not listed here, or several lights come on at once, treat it as red: stop safely and contact the fleet desk with the truck's fleet number and a photo of the instrument cluster.
